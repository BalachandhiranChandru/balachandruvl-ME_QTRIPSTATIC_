About the Experience

QCharley's Jade Resort is constructed in the most natural setting for a day out that pleases the senses. The property is spread over eight acres of land with facilities for both indoor and outdoor sports. For the indoor arena, we have a squash, table tennis, carrom, chess, billiards, and a snooker. Further, there is a host of popular games and adventures. If that is not enticing enough head to the gymnasium.

Inclusions
  • Swimming pool access
  • Outdoor sports: Water Polo, Archery, Tennis, and Zorbing
  • Indoor sports: Indoor Squash, Table Tennis, Carrom, Badminton, Chess, Billiards, and Hi-Tech 360 KINETIX
  • Trekking
Note
  • Some accessories like bats and shuttlecocks will need to be purchased
  • All adventures and game accessories are subject to availability
  • It is compulsory to wear swimwear for all indoor and outdoor swimming pool
  • Children must be accompanied by parents or guardians at all times
  • Complimentary entry for children below the height of three feet. A valid photo ID is needed to avail the discount
